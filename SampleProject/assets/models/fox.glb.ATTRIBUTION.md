# fox.glb

From the Khronos glTF Sample Assets repository, and kept here because it is
the one model in this project with **more than one animation clip** -- which
is what makes a cross-fade (7.5) something that can be looked at rather than
only asserted. Its three cycles are Survey, Walk and Run, and they drive the
same joints.
